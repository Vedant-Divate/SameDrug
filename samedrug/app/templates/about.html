About SameDrug

Disclaimers

Informational only — NOT medical advice. Always verify with a pharmacist or doctor before buying or switching medicines. A cheaper equivalent is not automatically suitable for you.

Ceiling prices © NPPA; Jan Aushadhi prices © PMBJP. SameDrug is not affiliated with either body. NPPA ceilings are tax-exclusive while Jan Aushadhi MRPs are tax-inclusive, so small differences may reflect tax, not real savings.

Methodology

NPPA ceiling rows and Jan Aushadhi products are joined on a canonical key of molecule + strength + form (salt suffixes and form variants normalized). Each join records its match method (exact, alias, fuzzy, modifier-relaxed, form-family) and a confidence level. Relaxed matches always carry a visible warning. Zero-MRP Jan Aushadhi rows (“price not yet published”) are never used in comparisons.

Shareable cards live at /d/{slug}, where the slug is derived from the canonical key. Of {{ n_slugs }} formulation slugs, {{ slug_collisions }} needed deterministic disambiguation.
